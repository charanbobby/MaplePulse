# MaplePulse — Business + Marketing Script (Read-Along)

**Length:** ~7 minutes
**Style:** Read out loud while screen recording. Copy-paste the examples into the app.

---

## INTRO (30s)

"You write a message. You think it sounds good. You send it out. Then you find out it didn't land.

That happens because we test our messaging on ourselves — or on people who think like us.

MaplePulse lets you test your message on AI personas built from real Canadian data — before you spend a dollar. Let me show you the full flow."

---

## THE APP (20s)

> **[SCREEN: Show landing page, click Get Started]**

"Two fields. Who you're trying to reach, and what you want to say. Three modes — Focus Group, A/B Test, Survey Pre-Test. I'll start with Focus Group and walk you through every step."

## FULL WALKTHROUGH — Costco Ad (3 min)

This is the hero demo. Show every screen.

> **[SCREEN: Copy-paste below, hit Run]**

```text
AUDIENCE: Costco shoppers in Canada
MESSAGE:  Now is the best time to become a member. At Costco, it's all about quality you can see and feel, brands you know and love, and services you can trust.
```

### Step 1 — Panel Builds (20s)

> **[SCREEN: Show panel cards appearing]**

"I typed 'Costco shoppers in Canada' and the system built the panel. Look — suburban families, retirees stocking up, small business owners buying in bulk. Different ages, incomes, provinces. Each one pulled from real census data. I didn't set any filters. The system figured out who shops at Costco."

### Step 2 — Reactions Stream In (25s)

> **[SCREEN: Show reactions appearing one by one. Highlight 2-3.]**

"Now each persona reads the ad and reacts. Watch them come in one by one. This one says 'quality you can see and feel' is vague — what does that actually mean? This one points out that 'now is the best time' doesn't say why now. Each reaction has a sentiment score, a tone fit rating, and flags."

### Step 3 — Review (15s)

> **[SCREEN: Show the review screen with checkboxes]**

"Before we go further, I can review the reactions. If any feel off, I uncheck them. The system also auto-flags low-quality ones. I'll keep most of these — they look solid. Hit continue."

### Step 4 — Summary (15s)

> **[SCREEN: Show the summary — average sentiment, tone distribution, flags]**

"Here's the summary. Average sentiment across the panel. Tone distribution — how many found it appealing versus generic. And the common flags. This is your dashboard. One screen, the whole story."

### Step 5 — Optimized Rewrite (20s)

> **[SCREEN: Show the optimization screen — original vs improved]**

"Now this is where it gets really useful. The system takes the reactions, figures out what fell flat, and rewrites the message. Look — it kept the membership pitch but made it more specific. Instead of vague words like 'quality you can feel,' it gives a concrete reason to join now. It tells you exactly what it changed and why."

### Step 6 — Round 2 (20s)

> **[SCREEN: Show round 2 reactions streaming in]**

"The same panel now reads the new version. Watch the scores — they're higher across the board. The flags dropped. The personas who were lukewarm before are responding better now."

### Step 7 — Before and After (25s)

> **[SCREEN: Show the final comparison — side by side, scores, improvement]**

"And here's the payoff. Original message on the left, improved on the right. Sentiment went up. Flags went down. You can see exactly what changed and how the panel responded.

That's the full loop — describe your audience, paste your message, get reactions, get a rewrite, and see the before and after. The whole thing took about 30 seconds."

## A/B TEST — Quick Demo (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Switch to A/B mode, copy-paste below]**

```text
AUDIENCE: Canadian homeowners aged 35-55, considering home renovation
VARIANT A: Your dream kitchen is closer than you think. Free quote in 24 hours.
VARIANT B: Stop overpaying for renovations. Compare 3 local contractors instantly.
```

"A/B mode. Two headlines, same panel, head to head. The system picks a winner and tells you why. Higher income people liked the aspirational one. Cost-conscious people preferred the practical one. Now you know which to run where."

---

## SURVEY PRE-TEST — Quick Demo (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Switch to Survey mode, copy-paste below]**

```text
AUDIENCE: Canadian parents with children in public school, K-12
QUESTION 1: How satisfied are you with your child's school? (1-5)
QUESTION 2: Do you feel the curriculum adequately prepares your child for the future?
QUESTION 3: Would you support increased property taxes to fund smaller class sizes?
```

"Survey mode. The panel reads each question and flags problems. Question three is leading — it only frames the answer as a tax increase. 'Adequately' in question two is vague. These are the problems that ruin your survey data, and they catch them before you send it out."
