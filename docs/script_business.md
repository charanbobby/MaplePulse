# MaplePulse — Business YouTube Script

**Target audience:** Marketers, product managers, founders, non-technical viewers
**Tone:** Conversational, honest, minimal jargon — do NOT oversell
**Length:** ~5-7 minutes

---

## INTRO (30s)

- Hook: "I built a tool that lets you pressure-test your messaging against AI personas grounded in real Canadian data — before you spend a dollar on a campaign"
- Be upfront: these are synthetic personas, not real people — this is a starting point, not a replacement for real research
- Introduce MaplePulse: synthetic focus group as a service for Canada

## THE PROBLEM (1 min)

- Traditional focus groups: expensive, slow, small sample, geographic bias
- Surveys: low response rates, hard to get nuanced reactions
- Gut feeling: most content decisions are still made this way
- The gap: there's nothing quick and cheap to sanity-check your messaging before you commit budget
- MaplePulse sits in that gap — it's a first filter, not the final answer

## WHAT MAPLEPULSE DOES (1.5 min)

- Step 1: Describe your target audience in plain English
- Step 2: System assembles a panel of AI personas grounded in real Canadian data
- Step 3: Get instant reactions — sentiment, cultural flags, tone fit
- Step 4: Get an optimized version of your message + before/after comparison
- [PLACEHOLDER: Screen recording walkthrough of the UI]

## HONEST LIMITATIONS (45s)

- These are AI-generated personas — they approximate real demographics, they don't replace real voices
- Good at catching obvious blind spots, cultural missteps, tone mismatches
- Not a substitute for real user research, customer interviews, or representative surveys
- Think of it as a spell-checker for your messaging — it catches things you'd miss, but you still need a human editor

## WHY THE DATA MATTERS (1 min)

- Personas built from 30+ real Canadian data sources (census, wages, surveys)
- Not generic AI — each persona has demographics, income, values, concerns grounded in real distributions
- Multi-model approach: multiple AI models react independently to reduce single-model bias
- Anti-sycophancy design: the system is built to give honest, critical feedback — not just tell you what you want to hear

- Content localization: Does this ad land differently in Quebec vs. Alberta?
- Product concept testing: Would suburban parents care about this feature?
- Ad copy A/B testing: Which headline resonates more?
- Survey pre-testing: Are these questions clear and unbiased?
