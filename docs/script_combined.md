# MaplePulse — YouTube Script (Read-Along)

**Length:** ~5 minutes
**Tone:** Casual, honest, straight to the point
**How to use:** Read this out loud while screen recording the app.

---

## HOOK (15s)

"You wrote a marketing message. Before you spend money putting it in front of real people — how do you know it's going to land?

I built a tool that answers that question. It's called MaplePulse."

---

## WHAT IT IS (30s)

"MaplePulse is a synthetic focus group for Canada.

You type in who you want to test with — like 'Costco shoppers' or 'new parents in Ontario' — and it builds a panel of AI personas that match.

These aren't random. Each persona comes from real Canadian data — census, wages, housing, cultural backgrounds. Over 30 public data sources.

You paste your message, hit run, and each persona reacts to it independently."

> **[SCREEN: Type "Costco shoppers" + a sample ad, hit Run, show panel appearing]**

---

## HOW IT WORKS (45s)

"There are three modes.

**Focus Group** — you give it one message, you get back reactions from the whole panel. It scores sentiment, flags cultural issues, tells you if the tone fits. Then it rewrites your message and runs the panel again so you can see a before and after.

**A/B Test** — you give it two versions of a message. Same panel reads both. You get a winner and you get the reasons why.

**Survey Pre-Test** — you paste your survey questions. The panel tells you which ones are confusing, biased, or culturally off."

> **[SCREEN: Click through the three mode tabs, show each input form briefly]**

---

## LIVE DEMO (2 min)

"Let me show you a few real examples."

### Example 1: Diwali Campaign (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Type audience + message, show results streaming in]**

"I'm testing a jewelry ad for Diwali. I just type 'Diwali marketing campaign for a jewelry brand' and the system figures out the right panel — South Asian Canadians, right cultural background, right age range.

Look at the reactions. One persona flags that 'gift the brilliance she deserves' is gendered. Another says 'inspired by tradition' feels performative. That's the kind of stuff you miss in a boardroom."

### Example 2: A/B Test — Renovation Headlines (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Switch to A/B mode, enter two headlines, show comparison]**

"Two headlines for a renovation company. 'Your dream kitchen is closer than you think' versus 'Stop overpaying for renovations — compare 3 local contractors.'

The panel picks a winner and tells you why. Higher income people liked the aspirational one. Budget-conscious people preferred the practical one. Now you know which to use where."

### Example 3: Survey Pre-Test (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Switch to Survey mode, paste 3 questions, show flags]**

"Three questions about school satisfaction. Watch — the panel catches that question three is leading. It frames the only solution as a tax increase. It also flags that 'adequately' in question two means different things to different people. These are the issues that ruin your survey data."

### Quick Hits (30s)

> **[SCREEN: Rapid-fire — just type audience briefs and show panels appearing]**

"And just to show you the range — Tim Hortons regulars. Filipino nurses in Alberta. Hockey parents in small-town Ontario. Gen Z renters in Vancouver.

Two words and you get a full panel. The system does the demographic work for you."

---

## HONEST TAKE (30s)

"I want to be clear about what this is and what it isn't.

These are AI personas. They're built on real data, but they're not real people. This is a first filter — like a spell-checker for your messaging. It catches blind spots and cultural missteps before you go live.

It's not a replacement for talking to real customers. It's the step you take before that."

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Total time:** ~5 minutes when read at a natural pace
- **Screen recording:** Split screen works well — your face on the side, app taking most of the space
- **Key moments to emphasize:** The panel building automatically from a plain-English brief, reactions streaming in one by one, the before/after comparison
- **Don't rush the demo sections** — let viewers see the reactions appearing
